Background

Who I am

Born in Kansas City. Raised in Shawnee, Kansas. B.S. Computer Science, minor in Physics - Kansas State University, May 2024. Today, I'm an Assistant Software Developer at Burns & McDonnell.

I discovered computer science in high school as a surprise. What I thought was "only for nerds" turned out to be the perfect intersection of logic and creativity. I spent my senior year building games in Unity with C#, and that hooked me.

College sharpened the focus. The first half deepened my C#; then Python and data science took over - and I haven't looked back. Database systems, software architecture, concurrent programming, algorithms - I wanted to understand it all.

Since graduating, I joined Burns & McDonnell's Aviation & Federal Global Practice. I sit alongside the architects and engineers in A&F every day, building internal tools that compress hours of work into seconds. My job is to listen, find the friction, and write software that makes it disappear. I believe the best engineers never stop being students - and every day in A&F teaches me something new.
